R080 Timer

Un timer per ingranditore da camera oscura che spegne la lampada al millisecondo esatto. Focus, Countdown e Test strip, tre canali che ricordano tutta l'impostazione di stampa, F-Stop, display rossi pensati per il buio.

A darkroom enlarger timer that switches the lamp off to the exact millisecond. Focus, Countdown and Test strip, three channels that remember the whole print setting, F-stop steps, red displays made for the dark.

  • Timing decided in hardwareTimer1 at 1 kHz is the only exposure clock and switches the relay off inside the interrupt.
